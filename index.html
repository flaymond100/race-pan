🏔 Alpe Gran Fondo — Race Day Plan

~130km · ~2,000m+ climbing · 9:00 Start · Est. ~4h

Race Day — 9:00am Start
130km · Major alpine climbing (route peaks ~1,340m) · Hardest climb in the final hour · Est. finish ~13:00
Performance day — no deficit
80–95g carbs/hour during race
~9–10 gels equivalent during race
⚠️ This is a 4-hour mountain event — fuelling is the #1 controllable At ~4h with 2,000m+ of climbing you will burn through glycogen completely. The route saves its hardest climb for the final hour (the long drag back up to ~1,340m). If carb intake slips in hours 2–3, you will blow up exactly when the race gets hardest. Treat fuelling as non-negotiable: eat to schedule, not to appetite.

7:30am

0.2–0.3 g/kg (14–22g) with 500ml water + 2–3 plain rice cakes. Peak buffering at ~9:00–9:30.
~7:45am
🍌 Small carb top-up
Banana or 2 rice cakes with jam (~30g) — keeps blood glucose stable through the wait.
8:00am
☕ Caffeine 200mg
60 min before. Peaks ~8:45–9:15 and half-life (3–5h) carries strong levels into the mid-race climbs.
8:45am
🧃 Gel #1 — pre-race (~25g carbs)
Non-caffeinated. CNS priming + tops off blood glucose.
9:00am
🚴 RACE START
Early gentle valley section — start fuelling early while the stomach can handle it.
~9:30am (30 min)
🧃 Gel #2 (~25g)
Don't wait until hungry. Take on a flat or gentle gradient.
~10:00am (60 min)
🧃 Gel #3 + water at feed
First big climb territory. Settle into a sustainable pace.
~10:30–11:30 (1.5–2.5h)
🧃 Gels #4, #5, #6 + real food
High climbing load. Mix gels with banana/rice cakes from feed stations. ~1 fuel hit every 25–30 min.
~11:30am (2.5h)
☕ Caffeinated gel (~25g + 50–100mg caffeine)
Tops up caffeine for the brutal final climb. Only if you've trained with caffeinated gels before.
~12:00pm (3h)
🧃 Gel #7 + electrolytes
Final long climb to ~1,340m begins. This is where the race is won or lost — keep carbs flowing.
~12:30–13:00 (final hour)
🧃 Gels #8–9 + sodium
Sodium priority in the final hour. Push through the summit, then the fast descent to the finish.

⚠️ Caffeine for a 4-hour race 200mg at 8:00am peaks early-race and its 3–5h half-life carries meaningful levels into the mid-race climbs. Because your hardest climb is in the final hour (~3h+ in), a second smaller hit via a caffeinated gel around 2.5h tops you up exactly when you need it. Total stays around 250–300mg — within safe range. Only do this if you've used caffeinated gels in training.
✅ No antibacterial mouthwash this morning Kills the oral bacteria needed to convert dietary nitrate → nitrite. Regular toothpaste only.
⚠️ Hydration on a long climbing day 500–750ml/hour depending on temperature. With 2,000m+ of climbing you'll sweat heavily on the lower slopes. Use every feed station to refill — running dry on the final climb is as costly as bonking.
