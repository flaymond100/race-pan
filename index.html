Friday — Travel + Carb Load Day
Depart 8:30am · Arrive ~3:00pm · Bib pickup 17:00 · Race brief 19:00
Carbs: 8–10 g/kg (~576–720g)
Protein: 1.4–1.6 g/kg (~100–115g) — reduced to make room
No deficit — full maintenance + carb loading
🏔 125km / 1,450m changes everything vs. the short race This race will take ~3.5–4.5 hours. You WILL deplete glycogen. This is one of the events where carb loading genuinely and meaningfully improves performance. Friday's loading is not optional — it's critical. Hit 8–10 g/kg even if it feels like a lot. Keep fat and fibre minimal all day.

⚠️ Cut-off warning — first 37km You must average 25 km/h for the first 37km or you get redirected to the short course. That's a hard 88-minute window. Don't go out too hot trying to make the cut — ride your pace and trust fitness.
⚠️ Caffeine strategy differs from shorter races For a 3.5–4.5h race, the 200mg taken at 10:00am with a half-life of 3–5h means meaningful caffeine levels persist all the way to the finish (~14:30–15:30). No need for caffeinated gels mid-race unless you feel mentally flat — adding more caffeine risks GI distress at race intensity.
✅ Race feed stations Road Classics typically provides bananas, rice cakes, and fluids at stations. Use them for water and light real food — it breaks up the gel monotony and provides sodium naturally. Don't overstop though.
