Thursday — Normal Training Day
2 days out · Modest caloric deficit okay · Normal training fueling
Carbs: 5–6 g/kg (~360–432g)
Protein: 1.8–2.0 g/kg (~130–144g)
Fat: moderate
~200–300 kcal deficit
⚖️ Weight loss strategy — Thursday Thursday is the day to apply a modest deficit. Two days out, it has zero impact on race performance but contributes to your long-term weight trajectory. Keep carbs adequate for training, prioritise protein to preserve muscle, and keep fat moderate. Do not deficit on Friday or Saturday.

Friday — Pre-Race Carb Day
1 day out · Increase carbs · Reduce fat & fibre · Light or rest day
Carbs: 7–8 g/kg (~504–576g)
Protein: 1.6 g/kg (~115g) — slightly reduced to make room for carbs
Fat: low
Maintenance calories — no deficit
⚖️ Weight loss strategy — Friday Friday is not a deficit day. Glycogen loading requires caloric headroom. Any deficit today directly compromises tomorrow's race performance. Accept the maintenance day — it will not derail your weight trajectory meaningfully. Shift from fat and protein to carbohydrates, and keep fibre low to avoid GI issues on Saturday.
