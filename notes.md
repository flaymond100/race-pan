## 5:30am — Breakfast

| Food | Carbs |
|---|---|
| 80g granola | ~55g |
| 1 large banana | ~27g |
| 2 tbsp jam (~40g) | ~28g |
| 150g skyr | ~8g |
| 200ml apple juice or sports drink | ~20g |
| **Total** | **~138g (1.9 g/kg)** ✅ |

> Add the juice/sports drink to hit the 1.5–2 g/kg target — granola and skyr alone fall slightly short. Note that granola has more fibre than white oats; if your gut is sensitive before races, keep the portion to 80g max.

**Also take with breakfast:**
| Supplement | Dose |
|---|---|
| 🌱 Nitrates (beetroot shots) | 2× concentrated shots (~12–16 mmol) |
| 💊 Creatine | Normal daily dose |
| 💊 Beta-alanine | Normal daily dose |

---

## 7:00am — Sodium Bicarbonate

| What | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Dose** | 0.2–0.3 g/kg = **14–22g** |
| **With** | 500ml water + small low-fibre snack (e.g. 2 rice cakes with jam ~20–25g carbs) |
| **Why now** | Peak blood buffering capacity at 60–90 min = right at race start |

> If you've had GI issues with bicarb before, stay at the lower end — **0.2 g/kg (14g)**. Taking it with food and water is essential to reduce stomach distress.

---

## 8:00am — Caffeine

| What | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Dose** | **200mg** |
| **Why now** | Peak plasma caffeine at 45–75 min = **8:45–9:15am**, covering the hardest final third of the race |

## Key Rules for Tomorrow

- ✅ Nothing new — only foods and products you have used before
- ✅ Keep fat and fibre minimal from now until after the race
- ✅ Set two alarms — missing the 5:30am breakfast window is the most common race day mistake
- ✅ No antibacterial mouthwash in the morning — it kills the oral bacteria needed to convert nitrate to nitrite
- ✅ Don't overdrink before the start — sip, don't gulp
